This is an example website: it shows how Houdini networks look when you embed them with NOP on your own page. Back to the NOP Playground

Sample article

Building a procedural circuit board in Houdini

Imagine this is your portfolio or blog. Below the text you would normally show a screenshot of your node network. With NOP your readers get the real network instead: they can move around, zoom in, open subnetworks and read every parameter and VEX snippet, on desktop and on their phones.

The setup

In the scene, the whole board comes from one asset driven by a curve. Click the circuit_gen node to see the parameters the asset exposes.

Inside the asset

Inside, the generator has 169 nodes. Readers can explore it at their own pace instead of squinting at a screenshot. Double-click circuit_board_fan to go into a nested asset, and use the path bar at the top to come back.

How this page does it

One script tag on the page, then one tag per network, pointing at the file exported from Houdini:

<script type="module" src="web-component.js"></script>

<houdini-network
  src="circuit_board-network.json"
  style="display: block; height: 480px"
></houdini-network>

The viewer draws inside its own Shadow DOM, so the styles of your site and of the network never mix. During the tester phase the script is not on npm yet; the Embed button in the playground has an iframe version that works today.
